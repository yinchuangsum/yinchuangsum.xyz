On July 23, about 40 builders packed into the AWS Office at The Gardens, KL for the second Malaysian AI Show & Tell. No slides, no sales — just 11 raw demos across 4 hours.

I presented **MachineIQ**, my capstone for the Gamuda AI Academy. The honest number: 99% of the code was generated by AI. The stack was OpenCode + Open Design + MCP skills (especially Brewie for requirement elicitation). I route heavy coding to DeepSeek and Kimi — Chinese models that are genuinely underrated in the local dev community — and reserve Claude/GPT for multi-modal reasoning and complex chains. This token routing cuts API spend by roughly 40–50% compared to running everything on frontier models.

The broader night surfaced four themes worth sharing: character consistency in GenAI video is not solved — anyone demoing video shows their best take, not their average; spec quality is the universal predictor of output quality in AI coding; subscriptions beat token-based billing for heavy users; and the Malaysian AI scene remains refreshingly skeptical, pragmatic, and open — no gatekeeping, no hype cycles.

The meetup is organised through [Luma](https://luma.com/dht6mvpw) — worth joining if you build with AI in Malaysia.
